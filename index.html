Pranaq

Carbon credits you can verify from orbit.

Pranaq checks tree-planting claims against Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, scores them with machine learning, and records approved credits on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet.

Live demo on the Sepolia test network. No real funds are involved.

How it works

Each project moves through the same four stages, from land claim to credits.

  1. Submit a land claimA developer registers the parcel and planting date.
  2. Satellite analysisNDVI, EVI and NDWI are measured from Sentinel-2 images.
  3. ML confidence scoreThe result is scored and marked pass or fail.
  4. Credits minted on-chainApproved credits are minted, then retired by buyers.
